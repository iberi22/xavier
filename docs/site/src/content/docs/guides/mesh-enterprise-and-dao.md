# Enterprise Brain, DAOs, & P2P Mesh Networks Guide

> Visual and operational manual for configuring enterprise knowledge meshes, DAO councils, and secure communications in Xavier Panel UI.

---

## 1. Creating a Mesh Network (Enterprise Brain)

1. Navigate to the **#mesh** tab in the top navigation bar.
2. Click **Create New Network** to open the setup wizard.
3. Choose the **Enterprise Brain** template:
   - Sets up hierarchical clearance levels (`PUBLIC`, `INTERNAL`, `RESTRICTED`, `CONFIDENTIAL`, `SECRET`, `TOP_SECRET`).
   - Configures private persistence sub-nodes to act under a unified organization identity.
4. Toggle **Host as Master Node** to make your current instance the primary anchor.
5. Generate an **Invite Ticket / QR Code** to share with employees.

---

## 2. Visual Topology & 1-Click Employee Offboarding

- The **Mesh Topology Graph** displays all connected nodes in real-time:
  - **Master Host**: Your primary coordinator.
  - **Persistence Sub-nodes**: Encrypted storage mirrors.
  - **Employee Nodes**: Scoped personal nodes with role-based access.
- **Immediate Revocation (Kill-Switch)**: Click the red **Disconnect** button next to any node. Xavier broadcasts a signed revocation notice that immediately purges restricted enterprise memory from the remote device.

---

## 3. Democratic DAO Councils & Quorum Voting

- The **DAO Governance Panel** enables 1-node-1-vote democratic decision making:
  - Create proposals to integrate public dataset bundles or change sync rules.
  - Endorsement badges (avales) verify node eligibility.
  - Cast **For**, **Against**, or **Abstain** ballots with live quorum calculation.

---

## 4. Orion Health Family Records & Read-Once Doctor Passes

- Manage clinical episodes and lab documents in **Family Health Records**.
- Generate temporary **Read-Once** or time-locked (1 hour) access tokens for doctor consultations.
- Access automatically self-destructs upon completion of the medical review.

---

## 5. P2P Encrypted Messaging & WebRTC Voice Calls

- **Direct Messages**: End-to-end encrypted chat between peer nodes.
- **Network Rooms**: Topic-based channels (`#general`, `#legal`, `#council`).
- **WebRTC Voice Calls**: Direct peer-to-peer audio calls with encrypted media streams.
